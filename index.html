One place, many lines of work

The workshop has grown.
The address has stayed.

In 1993 this website started with my Mercedes-Benz 450SEL 6.9. It did not begin as a software storefront or a list of repositories. Since then the interests have expanded, but they still belong together: how systems are designed, how machines behave and what remains useful over time.

Here, Workbench is where I explain the engineering, Projects is the complete GitHub source inventory, Garage records the vehicles, and About tells the longer story. The Software Centre is specifically for published packages.
