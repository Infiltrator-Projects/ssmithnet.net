One place, many lines of work

Find the work.

This began with my Mercedes-Benz 450SEL 6.9 in 1993. The work has since grown into filesystems, diagnostic tools and simulations, but I still want to understand how things behave and show what I can actually verify. Some questions become code; others remain machines worth documenting.

From an operating system to its parts.

InfiltratorOS coordinates distinct components. InfiltratorFS owns its on-disk format; Libraries defines shared interfaces; Boot, Kernel and Files each have a different job. Their boundaries are as important as the code.
