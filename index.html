Featured engineering · Software foundations

I build the parts below the interface.

InfiltratorFS is my own filesystem design. Libraries supplies common contracts; Boot and Files consume filesystem capabilities in different environments; InfiltratorOS brings the components together. Each project has its own source, tests and qualification limits.

It's the sort of work I enjoy most: finding out what each layer must do, making the boundaries explicit and testing what actually works rather than relying on a successful build.

Featured hardware · Open-reel audio

ReVox B77 Mk II + dbx 224 + Technics SU-Z400

My ReVox B77 Mk II, dbx 224 and Technics SU-Z400 represent three distinct parts of my analogue setup: tape transport, external Type II noise reduction and stereo amplification. The redrawn artwork includes the BASF tape reel and my silver metal reel. The Workbench has the equipment story.
